# Klue

Klue connects Claude to your team's competitive intelligence. Use it to compare
competitors, prepare for a deal, handle objections, investigate win/loss
patterns, and find buyer feedback with links to the underlying Klue sources.

Install the plugin, connect your Klue account through OAuth, then ask a seller
or competitive-intelligence question in plain language. The available content
and tools depend on your organization's Klue setup and your access permissions.
The bundled skills guide read-only research and seller advice; they do not
create, edit, or delete Klue content without your explicit request.
